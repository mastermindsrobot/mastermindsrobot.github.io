Est. 2023 · FIRST Robotics Competition

We build engineers. We build programmers. We build thinkers, builders, competitors, and the future leaders of the world.

Team 30458 is a community robotics team designing, building, and competing at competitions every season, starting with First Lego League (Aug. 2023 - Apr. 2025), and First Tech Challenge (Sept. 2025 - Present), mentoring the next generation of builders along the way.

What we do

Eight weeks to a working robot

Design → Build → Compete → Iterate → Repeat

Mechanical

CAD & Design

We design in CAD to create a robot that fits specific criteria about intaking, shooting, scoring, etc. to compete that season.

Programming

Autonomy & Controls

We use Java-based code, integrate sensors, and build autonomous routines and foolproof teleop controls in the midst of competitions.

Business

Outreach & Sponsorshisp

We set up fundraisers, attend community STEM events with a booth, and create social media accounts to share the team's journey with the public and any aspiring roboticists.
