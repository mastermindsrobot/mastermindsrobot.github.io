What we do

Eight weeks to a working robot

Design → Build → Compete → Iterate → Repeat

Mechanical

CAD & Design

We design in CAD to create a robot that fits specific criteria about intaking, shooting, scoring, etc. to compete that season.

Programming

Autonomy & Controls

We use Java-based code, integrate sensors, and build autonomous routines and foolproof teleop controls in the midst of competitions.

Business

Outreach & Sponsorshisp

We set up fundraisers, attend community STEM events with a booth, and create social media accounts to share the team's journey with the public and any aspiring roboticists.

This season

Meet this year's robot

Every September the game season starts, and every year we start at a prototyping table. The 2025-2026 season's robot was designed around speed and consistency — designed, built, and coded by students in eight weeks for the December competitions.

"JEFF" — 25-26 Robot

We built this robot on a mecanum chassis for speed and movement, utilizing a powerful intake system and a flywheel that operates with a 6000 rpm motor to shoot the artifacts into the goal with velocity and precision.
