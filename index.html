What we do

Six weeks to a working robot

Design → Build → Compete → Iterate

Mechanical

CAD & Fabrication

Students design in CAD, then machine, weld, and assemble the robot in our shop.

Programming

Autonomy & Controls

Java-based robot code, sensor integration, and autonomous routines written and tested by students.

Electrical

Wiring & Systems

Power distribution, motor controllers, and pneumatics wired to competition standards.

Business

Outreach & Sponsorship

Fundraising, community STEM events, and the team's award submissions and media.
