Build season timeline

Kickoff → Competition

Week 1

Kickoff & strategy

The season's game is revealed. The team analyzes the rules and settles on a robot strategy.

Weeks 2–3

Design & prototyping

CAD models and rapid prototypes are built and tested to validate the mechanisms.

Weeks 4–5

Fabrication & assembly

Parts are machined, wired, and assembled into a complete robot on the shop floor.

Week 6

Programming & drive practice

Code is finalized, autonomous routines tuned, and drivers get practice time before bag day.

Weeks 7+

Competition season

The team travels to regional events, iterating on the robot between matches.
