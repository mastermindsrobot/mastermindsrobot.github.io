About the team

Who we are

Team 30458 is a community FIRST Tech Challenge team based out of Carmel, Indiana. We design, build, program, and compete with a robot we build from scratch every season

Our mission

We want to build our skills in our individual interest areas of engineering, technology, programming, designing, etc., while providing a safe teamwork environment and sharing our experiences and the FIRST organization with our community to bring more kids to STEM and robotics.

Our history

We started as an FLL team in 2023 with four girls, two of whom have continued to build the team since then, recruiting more aspiring roboticists and making it to the worlds championship in 2025. Since then, we transitioned into an FTC team in 2025 and mobilized more people to the team, reaching the state level our first year in a new competitive environment. Presently, we continue to create a educating environment dedicated to programming, building, and outreach, competing at numerous competitions each season, aspiring for higher achievements and better robots.

How we work

Build season runs eight weeks from kickoff to competition at least two times a week for a total of at least six hours. Outside of build season, the team runs off-season projects, mentors younger students through robotics competitions or library events as outreach, and works on learning how to better program and build a proper functioning robot.

Every season

Build season timeline

Kickoff → Competition

Week 1

Kickoff

The season's game is revealed. The team analyzes the rules and settles on a robot strategy.

Weeks 2–3

Designing & Prototyping

CAD models and rapid prototypes are built and tested to find a good intake system, shooting mechanism, chassis, etc.

Weeks 4–5

Building & Wiring

Parts are built, wired, and assembled into a strong and functioning robot.

Weeks 6-7

Programming

Code is created, autonomous routines stabilized, and the machinery triple-checked for power and accuracy.

Week 8

Drive practice

Continuous practices by the two drivers, human players, and programmers to fix any bugs in the robot before the first competition.

Weeks 9+

Competition Season

The team travels to competitions, iterating on the robot between matches and competitions.
